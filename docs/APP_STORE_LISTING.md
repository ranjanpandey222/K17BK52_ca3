IS MY TREATMENT WORKING?

## App Review notes (paste into "Notes" for the reviewer)
Ebb is a menopause symptom tracker. All user data is stored locally on the device with SwiftData. The app has no account system, no server and no third-party SDKs.

Subscription: after onboarding, the user sees Apple's SubscriptionStoreView with a 1-week free trial (yearly or monthly). Restore Purchases is available on the paywall and in More → Settings → Subscription.

HealthKit (optional, asked during onboarding and in Settings): we read sleep analysis and Apple Watch sleeping wrist temperature to display them on the Today screen next to the user's symptoms, and we optionally write the symptoms the user logs (hot flashes, night sweats, mood changes, etc.) to Apple Health. HealthKit data is never sent off the device or used for advertising.

The app provides general wellness information and self-tracking only. It doesn't diagnose or recommend treatment, and directs users to their clinician.

To review the full app: complete onboarding (about 1 minute), start the free trial with a sandbox account, then use Today → Check in. History lets you add check-ins for past days so the Insights and Doctor report features can be seen (they need 7+ check-ins).
