## The short version
- Your symptom check-ins, treatments and notes are stored **only on your iPhone**.
- We **don't** have accounts or servers that receive your health information.
- We **don't** use advertising trackers or social media SDKs, and we **never sell** your data.
- You can **export** or **delete** all your data in the app at any time.

## What Ebb stores on your device
When you use Ebb, the following is saved on your iPhone, inside the app:
- Profile details you choose to enter: first name, birth year, and your answer about your last period
- Your daily check-ins: symptoms and how strong they were, optional hot flash counts, lifestyle factors (such as caffeine or exercise) and notes
- Treatments you log, with dose, schedule and reminder settings
- App settings, such as reminder times and whether the app lock is on

This information isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Patterns, summaries and the doctor report are calculated on your iPhone. On supported iPhones, the weekly summary uses Apple's on-device language model, which runs entirely on the device.

Your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include app data, protected by Apple's backup security. You control this in your iPhone settings.

## Apple Health (optional)
If you allow it, Ebb **reads** sleep analysis and Apple Watch sleeping wrist temperature to show them in the app, and **writes** the symptoms you log to Apple Health. Data from Apple Health is only used to provide these features. It's never used for advertising, never sold, and never sent off your device by Ebb. You can change access in the Health app at any time.

## Subscriptions
Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Notifications
Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No notification content passes through our servers.

## Information we may receive
If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and keep it only as long as needed.

## Sharing
We don't share or sell personal information. If you choose to share your doctor report or data export (for example by AirDrop, email or print), that's sent by you, to whoever you choose.

## Your choices and rights
- **Export:** Settings → Your data → Export my data
- **Delete:** Settings → Your data → Delete all my data (or delete the app)
- Because we don't hold your health data on servers, there's nothing for us to delete on our side. If you contacted support, you can ask us to delete those emails.

Depending on where you live (for example California or Washington State), you may have additional rights over consumer health data. Because Ebb doesn't collect your health data on its systems, those rights are met by the in-app export and delete options. Contact us with any request.

## Children
Ebb is intended for adults and isn't directed to children under 13 (or 16 where applicable).

## Security
Data is stored using iOS data protection. You can add a Face ID / passcode lock in Settings. No method is perfectly secure, but keeping data on your device greatly reduces risk.

## Not medical advice
Ebb is for tracking and education. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition and isn't a substitute for professional medical advice.

## Changes
If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date and, for material changes, tell you in the app. If we ever add any data collection (such as anonymous analytics), we'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label first.
